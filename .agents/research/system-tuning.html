karchy System Tuning

Nine small settings from Omarchy and CachyOS that karchy doesn't have yet. Each is one small config file: no program added, nothing running in the background. Leave one out and you keep Arch's default.

Each setting

1 · How long shutdown waits for a stuck service Changed

Arch today
Each service gets 90 seconds to stop before it's killed. One stuck service (often a network mount or a hung app) holds shutdown or reboot for a minute and a half.
Omarchy
5 seconds per service, and 5 seconds for your whole user session.
CachyOS
10 seconds per service; your user session keeps systemd's own limit.
The catch
Your VMs run inside your user session and ask for 15 seconds to shut down cleanly. Omarchy's 5-second session limit would cut a running VM off at shutdown, like pulling its power cord. CachyOS's version doesn't touch the session, so VMs get their 15 seconds.
Proposal
CachyOS's 10 seconds, without Omarchy's session file.

2 · Wrong passwords before a lockout Recommend

Arch today
3 wrong passwords lock your account for 10 minutes for sudo, password prompts and the text console.
The problem
karchy's lock screen counts into the same tally, and it allows 10 tries. Mistype three times at the lock screen, unlock on the fourth, and sudo and Settings' password prompts are refused for the next 10 minutes, with no hint why.
Change
10 tries everywhere, matching the lock screen. The 10-minute lock after that stays.
Trade-off
Someone guessing at an unlocked console gets 7 more tries. On a personal machine with full-disk encryption that's a small cost.

3 · Disk scheduler Changed

Arch today
On NVMe the kernel schedules nothing. During a big update, copy or build, the desktop's small reads wait behind the writes, and apps stutter or freeze for a moment.
Omarchy
One scheduler (Kyber) for every disk.
CachyOS
A scheduler per disk type: Kyber for NVMe, mq-deadline for SATA SSDs, BFQ for spinning drives (BFQ is much better for hard drives).
On your desktop
The NVMe system disk gets Kyber; the 3.6 TB SATA SSD gets mq-deadline. The Framework's NVMe gets Kyber.
Proposal
CachyOS's rules.

4 · How many files a program may open Recommend

Arch today
Programs start allowed 1,024 open files. Programs that need more can raise it themselves, but not all do.
Who hits it
Games under Wine/Proton with esync, IDEs and language servers, file watchers in dev servers, sync tools. Symptoms: "too many open files", crashes, or a watcher that silently stops noticing changes.
Change
65,536 to start with. The hard ceiling stays 524,288.
Trade-off
None you'd notice.

5 · Mount events per second Recommend

Arch today
systemd processes 5 mount events per second.
Change
30 per second.
Why
Your desktop mounts several btrfs subvolumes, a data disk and NAS shares at boot. The low limit can delay local disks and other boot steps for a moment. Most of the time you'd see no difference.

6 · mpv decodes on the graphics card Recommend

Arch today
mpv decodes video on the CPU: the same problem Brave had with YouTube.
Change
hwdec=auto-safe: mpv uses the GPU's decoder when it's known to be reliable for that video, and the CPU otherwise.
Why
Playback doesn't stutter when the CPU is busy, and the laptop's battery lasts longer during video.
Override
Your own ~/.config/mpv/mpv.conf wins.

7 · More GPG key servers Recommend

Arch today
GPG asks one key server. When it's down or slow, importing a signing key fails: some AUR builds, pacman-key --recv-keys, verifying downloads.
Change
Five well-known key servers, giving up on each after 4 seconds.
Trade-off
None.

8 · Thunderbolt and USB4 at the disk-password prompt Recommend

Arch today
The Thunderbolt/USB4 driver loads after the disk is unlocked, so a keyboard on a USB4 dock doesn't work at the password prompt.
Change
Load it before the prompt, only where the kernel has it.
For you
Matters on the Framework with a USB4 dock. Your desktop board has no Thunderbolt or USB4, so nothing changes there.

9 · Logout order Cosmetic

Arch today
At logout everything in the session is told to stop at once, so the bar and wallpaper vanish a split second before Hyprland closes.
Change
Hyprland stops first, then the rest.
Why
Polish only.
